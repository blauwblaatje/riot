    What can I upload?
    • Scoreboard game files (.json) from the Java CRG scoreboard: a single game, or a data export with many games. Times only come from these: jam lengths, time to lead, lineups, timeouts.
    • Statsbooks (.xlsx), WFTDA's template, as filled in after the game. Corrections live here, so the stats use the statsbook when a game has one.
    • A zip of any of those.

    A game in both is matched by tournament, date and game number (or its teams), and the two are compared.
